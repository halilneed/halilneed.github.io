03 Commands

From complaint
to launch plan.

01

/painradar:scan <topic>

The complaint radar: 3–7 themes with a transparent Signal score, at least two linked items per theme, solution paths and content ideas. Blocked sources are reported as blocked, and thin evidence is called weak — never padded.

02

/painradar:fit

Which theme is worth building: pain × money proof (TrustMRR) × competition gap × your own persona from devpersona, if present × effort × platform risk. Every score points at evidence; without a TrustMRR key, revenue figures are marked unverified — never invented.

03

/painradar:launch [repo|idea]

One primary channel with stated reasons, posting rules for Reddit, Hacker News and Product Hunt, SEO comparison pages, a TrustMRR listing step, a 30-day calendar and three content briefs — all grounded in the radar evidence.

04 Trust

What goes out —
and what never does.

painradar is the one module in this suite that goes online. That is exactly why this section is precise about it.

  • It fetches public sources only — Hacker News via Algolia, GitHub Issues search, App Store review RSS. Every fetch is in radar.mjs, one file you can read.
  • Your side never leaves the machine. Your persona card, your repo and your chat history are read locally and sent nowhere — the only thing that goes out is the search queries you chose.
  • No usernames in output. Items keep title, link, date and counts; an author field is never written.
  • Reddit is off by default — licence and rate-limit risk. If you enable it, a 403/429 stops the run and the report says "blocked".
  • Failure stays visible. An unreachable source appears in the source note with its HTTP status; missing evidence is reported as weak signal, never backfilled.
  • Optional keys raise limits, nothing more. GITHUB_TOKEN for search quota, TRUSTMRR_API_KEY for verified revenue; without them the tool still runs and marks unverified figures as unverified.
  • MIT. LICENSE — no account, no signup, no telemetry.

05 Cloud

painradar Cloud
is in design.

The plugin stays free and local — that is a promise, not a phase. Cloud is the optional layer for people who want the radar waiting for them in the morning:

  • Scheduled daily scans — with history and charts: which themes are new, growing, fading.
  • Telegram / Slack digest — the morning radar delivered where you already read.
  • White-label reports — for agencies running the radar for their clients.
